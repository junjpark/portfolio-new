JobFinder SMS Terms

Last updated November 13, 2026

  1. Program: JobFinder sends texts summarizing newly posted entry-level job openings found on public company career pages, plus a confirmation when you reply with reference numbers.
  2. Frequency: up to 24 messages per day; usually fewer.
  3. Cost: message and data rates may apply.
  4. Opt out: reply STOP at any time. You'll get one confirmation and no further messages. Reply HELP for help, or email junjpark02@gmail.com.
  5. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  6. Privacy: see https://junjpark.vercel.app/jobfinder/privacy.
