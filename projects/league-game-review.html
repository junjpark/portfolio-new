Overview

Chess.com’s game review shows how each move changed your odds of winning. This project brings that idea to League of Legends by estimating the win probability at every minute of a ranked match.

the problem, who it was for and why it mattered

What I built

  • Collected ~20K ranked matches (~600K minute-by-minute game states) through a rate-limited, cached API client, validated 100% against official data.
  • Trained a calibrated XGBoost win-probability model that beats logistic-regression and neural-net baselines.
  • Shipped behind a 156-test CI suite with data-leakage checks and a run-once holdout gate.

Outcome

  • ~20Kranked matches collected
  • 0.8 pterror on 5.7K unseen matches
  • 156tests in CI
